# Code of Conduct

We want LearnSphere AI to be a friendly, respectful community for students and developers.

- Be respectful and inclusive.
- No harassment, hate speech or personal attacks.
- Give and accept feedback constructively.
- Help newcomers.

Violations can be reported to the maintainer through GitHub: [@amrut4469-blip](https://github.com/amrut4469-blip).
