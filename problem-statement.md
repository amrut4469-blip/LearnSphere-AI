# ❗ Problem Statement

## Background
Millions of students in India prepare for school boards, university exams (such as SPPU) and competitive tests. Most of them study from their **own class notes, syllabus and previous-year papers**.

## The gap
| Problem | Impact on the student |
|---|---|
| Question banks and apps are **generic** | Practice does not match the teacher's notes or the university pattern. |
| General chatbots are **ungrounded** | Answers come from the internet, so they may not match the syllabus. |
| Study tools feel **boring** | Students lose consistency and skip revision. |
| Dashboards show **fake or sample data** | Students cannot trust their own progress view. |
| Tools are **scattered** | Notes, doubts, practice and revision live in different places. |

## Who is affected
- School students preparing for board-style exams
- College students (e.g. SPPU semester exams)
- Competitive-exam aspirants

## Problem in one line
> Students need an AI study partner that learns from **their own material**, keeps them **engaged**, and shows **honest progress**.
