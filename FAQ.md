# ❓ FAQ

**Does LearnSphere AI use my own notes?**
Yes. Study material is generated from the document you upload.

**Does it show fake progress or sample questions?**
No. The product is designed to avoid sample questions and invented stats.

**Which files can I upload?**
PDF, DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX, TXT, CSV, XLS, XLSX, JPG and PNG, up to 50 MB.

**Can it read handwritten questions?**
Yes, use *Scan a question with AI* with the live camera or gallery.

**Which exams are supported?**
School, College (e.g. SPPU) and Competitive Exams modes.

**Is the AI always correct?**
No AI is perfect. Treat it as a study aid and verify important facts with textbooks or teachers.

**Which AI model is used?**
Google Gemini.

**Is it free to use?**
Try it here: https://learnsphere-ai-seven.vercel.app
