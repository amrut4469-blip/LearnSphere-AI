# 🎬 3-Minute Demo Script

**Live app:** https://learnsphere-ai-seven.vercel.app

| Time | What to show | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 – 0:20 | Dashboard | "Students study from their own notes, but tools are generic. LearnSphere AI fixes that." |
| 0:20 – 0:50 | **Set up exam**: SPPU, subject DS | "We start with the real exam and subject." |
| 0:50 – 1:20 | **Upload** `DataStructure Notes.pdf` | "The student uploads their own material. Gemini uses only this document." |
| 1:20 – 1:50 | **AI Assistant / AI Tutor** | "Doubts are answered from the uploaded notes, step by step." |
| 1:50 – 2:10 | **Scan a question** (camera or gallery) | "Even a handwritten question can be scanned and explained." |
| 2:10 – 2:35 | **Practice Arena + Learning Quests** | "Studying feels like a game, with questions made from their own notes." |
| 2:35 – 2:50 | **Revision Centre + Progress Analytics** | "Honest progress, no invented stats." |
| 2:50 – 3:00 | Closing | "LearnSphere AI: your syllabus, your notes, your preparation." |

## Tips
- Keep the sample PDF ready before the demo.
- Check the internet connection and the Gemini key.
- Have a backup screenshot: `assets/dashboard.png`.
