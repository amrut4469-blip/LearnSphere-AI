# ✨ Feature Guide

## 🤖 AI Studio
- **AI Assistant** – ask doubts, get answers from your own notes.
- **Video Notes Generator** – concise, revisable video-style notes.
- **Voice AI Tutor** – learn through spoken conversation.

## 🎓 Learning Space
- **Dashboard** – exam setup, upload, scan-a-question.
- **School Mode** – board-style learning.
- **College Mode** – university syllabus (e.g. SPPU).
- **Competitive Exams** – entrance and competitive prep.

## 🎮 AI Learning
- **AI Tutor** – step-by-step explanations.
- **Learning Quests** – missions that gamify study.
- **Practice Arena** – questions generated from your material.
- **Revision Centre** – smart last-minute revision.

## 📊 Your Progress
- **Progress Analytics** – real activity only.

## 📸 Smart Input
- **Scan a question** with live camera or gallery.
- **Upload** PDF, DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX, TXT, CSV, XLS, XLSX, JPG, PNG (up to 50 MB).
